Chenghong Li

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at Tsinghua University, collaborating with Prof. Yebin Liu. I received my Ph.D. degree from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Xiaoguang Han. Even earlier, I obtained my master's degree from Xi'an Jiaotong University and completed my undergraduate studies at Jilin University.

My research interests focus on Computer Vision and Graphics. Currently, I am focused on building generative world models for Embodied AI, building upon my background in human-centric computer vision and motion learning.
